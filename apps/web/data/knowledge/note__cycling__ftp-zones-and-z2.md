## FTP as the bike threshold

Functional Threshold Power (FTP) is the highest power sustainable for about an hour. PeteCoach uses FTP (when set) for zone targets and race projection. Without FTP, load falls back to heart rate.

## Establishing FTP (knee-aware)

Prefer tests that do not require a hard run afterward:

- 20-minute all-out indoor or outdoor effort × 0.95 as a practical estimate
- Or a progressive ramp to estimate threshold with a coach/protocol the athlete trusts
- Skip max testing during flares; maintain aerobic bike volume instead

Record the result in benchmarks/thresholds so analytics own the number.

## Working zone sketch ( fractions of FTP )

| Zone | % FTP | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Z2 aerobic | ~65–75% | Bulk of bike volume |
| Tempo / sweet spot | ~76–90% | Limited, deliberate |
| Threshold | ~91–105% | Rare quality |
| VO2 / anaerobic | >105% | Sparing use in build |

Exact boundaries can be tuned; the coaching point is **most riding stays genuinely aerobic**.

## Olympic bike application

Course character: urban Chicago — DuSable / Lower Wacker / busway. Expect turns, surfaces, and drafting rules per the year's race bible. Training implications:

- Practice controlled power through corners rather than only straight-road TT posture.
- Long Z2 rides build durability without the impact of long runs.
- Brick: finish rides with a short easy run only when the knee allows; otherwise do T2 rehearsal without impact.

## Strength interaction

Heavy lower-body strength (Rønnestad pattern) supports cycling economy. Keep hard strength and hard bike days separated enough that neither becomes junk fatigue.
