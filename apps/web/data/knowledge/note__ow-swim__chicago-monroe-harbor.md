## Course context

Olympic-distance swim: **1500 m** in Monroe Harbor. Start is typically early morning (race ~06:00). Water is often cool enough for a **wetsuit-legal** swim in late August, but confirm the year's temperature rules. Harbor swimming means chop from boats and wind, limited sight lines to buoys, and cold shock risk at entry.

## Priority skills (knee-safe, high ROI)

1. **Sighting rhythm** — every 6–8 strokes; lift eyes only, keep hips high. Practice in the pool with "alligator eyes" drills before OW sessions.
2. **Drafting** — legal and free speed; practice sitting on hips/feet without constant contact. Saves more time than most fitness gains.
3. **Wetsuit pacing** — expect faster CSS-equivalent pace in a well-fitting suit; do at least two full wetsuit continuous 1500 m sims before race week.
4. **Start composure** — controlled entry, no sprint into anaerobic debt in the first 200 m unless pack dynamics force it.
5. **Exit to T1** — practice standing, zipper pull, and the barefoot run into transition (see transitions note).

## Session templates

- Harbor continuous: 20–40 min easy OW with sighting focus.
- Race-pace blocks: 3–5 × 300–400 m at goal 1500 pace with easy swim recoveries.
- Wetsuit brick: OW swim → run-off into T1 rehearsal (short).

## Knee and safety constraints

- Prefer OW over run volume when medial symptoms are elevated.
- Never solo OW without a spotter/kayak when water is cold or choppy.
- Cap kick intensity if hamstring/pes anserine is irritable; rely on pull and body roll.

## Goal translation

Swim budget for sub-3: roughly **31–34 minutes**. Build continuous pace from CSS, then add 2–5% for chop/sighting until OW data replaces the estimate.
