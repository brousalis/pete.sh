# Race week — Chicago August heat and taper

- **Authors:** PeteCoach coaching note
- **Year:** 2026
- **Type:** note
- **Source:** Original synthesis for Supertri Chicago Olympic (Sunday 22 Aug 2027, ~06:00 start)
- **DOI / URL:** Chicago lakefront / urban course; mid-August humidity and heat risk

---

## Environment

Late August in Chicago can be warm and humid by mid-morning even with a 06:00 start. The swim may be cool; the bike and run can heat up quickly on asphalt and busway sections. Plan cooling and fluid strategy from weather, not optimism.

## Taper principles

- Cut **volume** more than intensity; keep a little sharpness.
- Protect sleep and PT compliance — Morning Activation / Evening Armor still happen.
- No new shoes, new wetsuit, or untested nutrition in race week.
- Same-day downgrades if symptoms spike; the race is not worth a layoff.

## Heat and hydration

- Pre-hydrate in the days before; do not chug excess the morning of.
- On-bike: fluids early and often; practice bottle plan on course-sim rides.
- Run: accept a slightly conservative pace if heat index is high — sub-3 dies from a blow-up more often than from a 30–60s conservative opener.
- Light colors, ice if available at aid, and a rehearsed hat/visor choice.

## Final rehearsals

- Swim: short OW or pool race-pace touches; one wetsuit reminder if legal.
- Bike: openers only; rack logistics mental walkthrough.
- Run: strides or a short shakeout, not a test.
- Transitions: one quiet dress rehearsal of kit layout.

## Morning-of

- Arrive early enough for the long T1 barefoot path without rushing.
- Warm-up that raises temperature without dumping matches.
- First 200 m of swim: controlled. First bike km: settle. First run km: patience.

## If heat or knee intervenes

Adjust goals in priority order: finish healthy, then time. A smart adjustment mid-race beats a DNF from stubbornness.
