## Why transitions matter here

At this venue, **T1 includes a long barefoot run** from swim exit to the bike racks. That stretch is free race time if rehearsed and expensive if improvised. Goal budgets: T1 ~6–8 min, T2 ~2–3 min. Improving transitions often beats adding more Z2 volume when the calendar is tight and the knee is limited.

## T1 checklist (swim → bike)

1. Cap and goggles off while moving.
2. Wetsuit peel to waist on the run, full strip at the strip zone / mat.
3. Barefoot run: short, quick steps; protect plantar surface; do not sprint if footing is rough.
4. Helmet on before touching the bike.
5. Shoes: decide rack-mount vs carry based on surface; practice both once.
6. First kilometre on the bike: settle breathing, drink, do not chase watts until HR settles.

## T2 checklist (bike → run)

1. Shoes off before dismount line if using bike-mounted shoes.
2. Rack bike, helmet off.
3. Run shoes + race belt + (optional) hat.
4. First 800 m: controlled cadence; ignore ego surges that spike medial load.

## Rehearsal sessions (high priority, low injury cost)

- Monthly: full T1 rehearsal after a pool or OW swim (include the barefoot segment length estimate).
- Brick: bike 30–60 min easy → T2 practice → 10–20 min easy run.
- Race month: two dress rehearsals with exact kit layout.

## Coaching rules

- Transitions are mandatory plan items, not optional fluff.
- Do not cut PT blocks to make time for transition practice; stack them around.
- If knee pain rises on the barefoot run segment, shorten stride and slow down — the time cost of caution is smaller than a flare.
