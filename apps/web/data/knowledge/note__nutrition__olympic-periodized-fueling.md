## Standing nutrition position

The previous deficit / delayed-eating / zero-carb-Sunday protocol is **retired**. At ~10–11% body fat with a cartilage and tendon rehab context, low energy availability risks impairing healing, hormonal health, and consistency. Default is **periodized maintenance**, not a cut.

The 170–175 lb band is a **constraint to hold**, not a target to grind toward via restriction.

## Practical targets

- **Protein:** ~1.6–2.2 g/kg/day, distributed across meals.
- **Carbohydrate:** scale with session demand — more around key swim/bike/run days, less on true rest days, never chronic zero-carb.
- **Collagen + vitamin C:** before strength and PT sessions when using that protocol.
- **Fluids / electrolytes:** especially for Chicago heat and long Z2 days.

## Around key sessions

- Pre: enough carbohydrate to perform the work quality prescribed.
- Intra (long bike / OW / bricks): practice race products, not experiments on race day.
- Post: protein + carbohydrate within a reasonable window; prioritize consistency over perfect timing.

## REDs red flags to watch

Persistent fatigue, declining performance despite rest, frequent illness, mood changes, stalled healing, or drive to under-eat relative to training load. If those appear, raise energy availability first and involve the MD/PT — do not push harder training.

## Coaching rules

- Do not prescribe a calorie deficit.
- If the athlete drifts back toward restriction, name the conflict with knee healing and sub-3 consistency.
- Race fueling is a trained skill: rehearse gut tolerance on long sessions in build.
